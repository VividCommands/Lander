Your setup. Without the guesswork.

Good settings should not be hard to use

Lonware started because setting up a PC, controller, or connection should not mean sorting through hours of conflicting advice. The useful changes were buried inside videos, old posts, and menus most people never needed to learn.

We tested what actually made a difference, removed what did not, and turned the results into tools that are straightforward to use. Macro.io came from the same idea. Give people real control without making the interface a fight.

Every Lonware product is built to make one part of your setup easier to understand, change, and come back to whenever you need it.

Clarity over clutter

Features and instructions should explain themselves. If a step is confusing, the product is not finished.

Control stays with you

You choose what to apply, and we provide a practical way to stop, change, or reverse it when appropriate.

Feedback becomes work

Customer reports are treated as product input. Repeated friction becomes a fix, not another support script.

Useful beats impressive

We would rather ship a dependable tool with a clear purpose than pad the lineup with features nobody needs.

Small enough to listen

Lonware grows by listening to the people who use it. Customer questions, different PC builds, controller issues, and real bugs show us exactly where a product needs more work.

That feedback matters more than a perfect benchmark headline. The goal is simple. Your setup should feel better, and you should understand what changed.
