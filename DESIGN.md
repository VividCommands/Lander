# Lonware product direction

Follow the owner's November 12 corrections. Website work only: Payhip products, names, visibility, pricing settings, and delivery files are outside this update. Preserve the existing direct checkout destinations. The Cloudflare checker and upgrade popup remain cancelled.

Keep Lonware's navy background, Rajdhani titles, Montserrat feature copy, illuminated outlines, and compact product presentation. Restore the corner planet and radial background depth. Refine the existing identity without replacing it with a generic flat comparison panel.

Macro opens with its name and “Keyboard and controller macros.” Keep the original planet and all automatic galleries. Reduce the hero from 1100px to 940px for the IQ composite and from 528px to 460px for individual windows. Increase the space between the gallery and the cards. Original image files remain unchanged. No captions or visible gallery controls are added.

The purchase cards use deep navy surfaces instead of the rejected gray/green treatments. Keep the subtle lit outlines the owner liked in the original Lite card. Lifetime has an ice blue badge with dark text and blue feature checks; Pro has a warm badge, orange Pro word and gold additions. The badges contrast with the card surfaces. Actual screenshots sit directly on the cards, without inset backgrounds, frames or shadows.

Make the content hierarchy explicit: Included features introduces Lifetime's six core feature entries. Only in Pro introduces the five existing Pro feature groups, with a plus marker, a stronger feature name and supporting detail where useful. State that all Lifetime features are also included. Do not introduce new capability claims. Keep the large Rajdhani product names and readable Montserrat feature text.

Prices and actions share a compact footer. Lifetime uses a blue outlined Buy Lifetime button; Pro uses a solid warm Buy Pro button. The buttons are 44px high, fit their labels, and have no bevel, gradient or moving shine. The purchase footer wraps naturally on small screens. Lifetime needs only its price and Lifetime access label; Pro keeps both its first-month price and monthly renewal amount.

The Products Macro screenshot is limited to 80% of the media width and 144px in height. Remove its drop shadow, padding and any CSS background. Load its scoped stylesheet after the old package styles so the intended natural proportions win the cascade.

Use every supplied screenshot: five Lifetime screenshots, fourteen original Pro screenshots, and the newer IQ composite. The hero and home Macro showcase cycle all twenty. The Lifetime card cycles five; Pro cycles fifteen. Main rotation takes five seconds, while the card galleries take roughly seven. Predecode the next image before replacing the current one. Keep the image area stable as differently sized screenshots rotate. Hover and keyboard focus do not stop autoplay. Holding the image pauses it until release; leaving focus or the window clears a held state. Hidden browser tabs suspend timers.

The home product carousel retains its existing product navigation. Each product stays forward for 15 seconds and its images change every 5 seconds, with the image timer starting when that product comes forward. Only the active product's Optii/PingR gallery advances. Preserve image aspect ratios and center images inside the available width and height, so SVG masks apply to the image itself rather than a letterboxed wrapper. The Products page uses the controller Drag Edit screenshot with a transparent media area.

Preserve the prior Optii exact-window mask and the PingR palette/asset cleanup. This correction does not redesign either page. Macro and PingR continue to omit the particle animation; Macro's static planet provides depth.

Website prices remain $15 for Lifetime and $24.99 for the first month of Pro, with $9.99 monthly renewals. Do not imply automatic recurring billing. Preserve xKhTB for Lifetime and NZ9tG for Pro. No merchant account changes belong in this task.
